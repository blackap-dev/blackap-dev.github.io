How MyWineTaste handles your diary, lists, photos and external services. This policy covers the Android app; the website has a separate notice.

At a glance. No MyWineTaste account is required. Your diary, lists and photos are handled on your device. Backups and sharing can transfer content to destinations you choose. Advertising and purchases use Google services, with data processing separate from your local diary.

1. Controller and contact

The data controller is Alberto Paolicelli, developer of MyWineTaste under the public name BLACK A.P. For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, contact blackap.dev@gmail.com.

2. Diary and local data

The app stores the information you enter on your device: wine names, wineries, vintages, dates, ratings, impressions, preferences, bottle details, grapes, shops, prices, lists and photographs. It also keeps your language, appearance, currency and usage preferences.

There is no MyWineTaste account or automatic upload of your diary to BLACK A.P. servers. Local processing lets you display, search, sort and edit your information.

Advertising and consentGoogle services, as explained below.

3. Photos and permissions

You can take or select label photos. The app uses the camera when you start taking a photo and imports a copy of your selected image into its storage. Images are resized for use in the app. Selecting an image does not delete the original from your gallery.

You can manage permissions in device settings. MyWineTaste does not request permission for precise GPS location.

4. Backup, export and sharing

A manual backup includes the database, lists, photos and the preferences included by the app. Restoring replaces current data and keeps a local safety copy of the previous state. CSV files contain text data without photos. Sharing cards contain the information shown in the preview and optional details you enable.

If you save a file to Drive or another service, or share it through email, messaging or social apps, the chosen provider receives that content. These transfers are initiated by you and are also governed by the recipient service’s terms and privacy policy. The app does not password-protect backup and CSV files.

Any automatic Android system backups or restores depend on your device and account settings. Copies saved outside the app remain at those destinations until you delete them there.

5. Google services

AdMob advertising

The Free version may show Google AdMob banners. Google advertising components may process IP addresses, approximate location inferred from IP, device identifiers, app and ad interactions and diagnostics for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. MyWineTaste does not include diary content in advertising requests.

Consent choices are presented where required. You can revisit them through Ad privacy choices in Settings when available. Full removes banners. See Google’s Privacy Policy and Google’s use of data from partner services.

Google Play purchases

The Full unlock uses Google Play Billing. The app checks purchase status and identifiers and stores the information needed to recognise your edition locally. It does not ask you to enter card details in the app. Payment, orders and refunds are handled through Google Play and related Google services.

Restoring purchases restores Full; recovering tastings, lists and photos requires their backup.

6. Purposes and external services

Data is used to provide requested features, handle support and purchases, meet applicable obligations and protect the service from abuse. Support processing responds to your request. Where applicable, processing relies on providing the service, legal obligations or a legitimate interest in security. Consent is requested for advertising processing that requires it.

Google, the email provider and services you choose for file storage or sharing process information under their respective roles and policies. Their services may involve processing outside your country, including outside the European Economic Area. Consult their policies for applicable transfer conditions and safeguards. The information website is hosted by GitHub Pages.

7. Retention and deletion

Retention depends on the data category and where the information is stored.

Retention criteria by data category
CategoryPeriod or criterion
Local diary, lists and preferencesRemain in app storage until you edit or delete them, or clear app data. Earlier information may remain in the safety copies described below.
Photos in app storageRemain while used by at least one tasting or list item. Cleanup removes app-managed photos with no remaining references. Original gallery images are separate.
Temporary backup, CSV and sharing filesRemain in the cache until cleanup. During later operations, the app’s cleanup functions attempt to remove recognised temporary copies older than seven days. This is not a guaranteed automatic deletion on day seven; the system may clear the cache sooner.
Local pre-restore safety copyA copy of the previous state is retained and accessible from Settings. A later restore may replace it; copies needed after an interrupted recovery remain as needed for that recovery. Clearing app data removes internal copies.
Exported files and shared contentCopies outside the app remain at your chosen destination until you delete them or the provider removes them. MyWineTaste cannot delete them remotely. Recipients may also retain their own copies.
Full verificationLocal verification information is used to recognise your purchase. It may be removed following a Google Play check that no longer confirms Full, or when app data is cleared. Google’s own order records follow Google’s retention rules.
Support emailsRetained while the request is open and to handle directly related follow-up. Unnecessary data and attachments are deleted when the matter is closed; further retention is limited to information needed for open disputes or applicable legal obligations, until those end or expire.
External provider dataGoogle and services you choose for email, storage or sharing apply their own periods and criteria as described in their policies. Deleting local app data does not automatically delete data held by these providers.

You can remove local data through Android’s clear app data function or by uninstalling the app, subject to any system backups or restores. Exports and shared content must also be deleted from their respective destinations. Deleting your diary does not cancel a Google Play purchase.

8. Support and rights

Where applicable, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or object to processing, and withdraw consent without affecting earlier lawful processing. Contact blackap.dev@gmail.com. You may complain to your competent supervisory authority; in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.

BLACK A.P. does not hold a remote copy of your local diary. You can manage it in the app and through your backup files. For data independently handled by a provider, also use the channels described in its privacy policy.

9. Audience

MyWineTaste is intended for adults interested in recording wine tastings and is not designed for children. Please contact us if you believe you have inadvertently sent us a child’s personal data.

10. Changes

We may update this page as features or services change. The date above identifies the latest version. New processing that requires consent will require the choices prescribed by applicable law.
