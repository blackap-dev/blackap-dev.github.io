Frequently asked questions

Before you start.

Where can I get the app?

The Google Play release is being prepared. We will add the official link when it is available. For information, email blackap.dev@gmail.com.

Do I need a MyWineTaste account?

No. Your diary and lists are saved on your device. Google Play purchases and any services you choose to store backups use their respective accounts.

Can I save a backup to Drive?

You can choose an available destination in your phone’s file picker, including a supported external service. Automatic cloud backup is not currently included.

How can I get support?

Email blackap.dev@gmail.com with your phone model, app version and a description of the issue. Attach only what is needed to explain it.
